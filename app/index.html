How it works

  1. Sample — 16 evenly-spaced frames are decoded from your video.
  2. Detect — a BlazeFace model (MediaPipe, also on-device) locates the largest face in each frame.
  3. Hear — the video's audio track is decoded in-browser and scored by a 1D-CNN trained to detect synthetic speech (TTS/voice-clone artifacts).
  4. Fuse — visual and audio branches combine into the final verdict. Everything stays on your device.
  5. Aggregate — frame scores are mean-pooled into a video-level verdict (the aggregation that scored best in our benchmark: AUC 0.9553 vs 0.9456 for the temporal transformer).
